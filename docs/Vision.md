
## Why and What?

 Whenever someone builds something, be it a product;idea or anything, the first question is "Why?", Why am I building Syntrophos? Well, we all use AI tools for a lot of purposes, from studying to coding,cooking,trip ideas,fitness plans,etc like they have become a daily part of life. But we use a lot of different LLM's for it and so the context goes missing and plus juggling through progress and past data, Syntrophos adresses exactly that; It is a personal project of mine to integrate my progress and metrics of life into a single umbrella, that umbrella is Syntrophos.
 
## How?

The next obvious question would be, "How does Syntrophos work?"

Well it works by continuously transforming information into understanding and understanding into action;

Other productivity apps like say Notion or your normal note takers/ habit trackers will simply store notes and tasks, Synthropos actively connects the information across your digital life,reasons about it using AI,and ultimately helps you make better decisions (Its that simple)
